How Does DentaBiome Work?

DentaBiome leans on postbiotic byproducts to encourage a balanced mouth environment while plant-sourced calcium with vitamins D3 and K2 supports the mineral side of enamel health.

1

Postbiotics set the tone

The postbiotic complex introduces stable bacterial byproducts that help encourage a favorable balance among the microorganisms living in your mouth.

2

Minerals go to work on enamel

Calcium from plant sources, together with vitamins D3 and K2, is included to support mineral availability and normal absorption so your enamel gets the raw material it needs.

3

Plant compounds defend and refresh

Consistency compounds the effect

Because oral balance shifts slowly, the formula is designed as a daily habit — taken regularly alongside brushing and flossing for ongoing support.

What Are The Ingredients In DentaBiome?

Each ingredient is listed individually so you can see exactly what is inside.

1

Postbiotic Complex

Heat-stable metabolic byproducts of beneficial bacteria — the signature ingredient of DentaBiome — included to help support a balanced oral microbiome environment alongside daily brushing.

2

Calcium (plant-sourced)

A plant-derived mineral source included to help supply the building blocks associated with tooth enamel strength and hardness.

3

Vitamin D3

A fat-soluble vitamin commonly paired with calcium to support normal mineral absorption and utilization in the body.

4

Vitamin K2

Often combined with D3 and calcium, K2 is included to help direct minerals toward where the body needs them, supporting the enamel-health angle of the formula.

5

Plant-Based Polyphenols

Plant compounds from botanical sources included for antioxidant activity that helps protect oral tissues from everyday oxidative stress.

6

Green Tea Extract

A well-known plant extract rich in catechins, included to help support a fresh, balanced mouth environment.

7

Xylitol

A plant-derived sugar alcohol used to sweeten the capsule formula while being a familiar ingredient in oral-care products for its mouth-friendly properties.

8

Peppermint Extract

A natural botanical extract that contributes a clean, fresh feeling and rounds out the plant-based profile of the formula.

9

Magnesium Stearate (plant-based)

A plant-sourced processing aid used in small amounts to keep the powder flowing evenly during capsule filling.

10

Vegetable Cellulose Capsule

A plant-based capsule shell that delivers the formula without gelatin, keeping the product suitable for plant-conscious users.
